Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Welcome to TaskFlow AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled within our mobile application.

1. Information Collection & Storage

TaskFlow AI is designed as a fully **offline-first** utility. All data you enter into the application, including:

  • User Account Credentials (encrypted locally)
  • Tasks, Descriptions, Categories, and Priorities
  • Reminder settings and times
  • Productivity statistics and logs

is stored strictly on your local device using secure asynchronous storage (AsyncStorage). We **do not** collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers, cloud environments, or third parties.

2. System Permissions

To provide core functionalities, the application requests the following permissions on your device:

  • Notifications: Required to trigger local alarms and alert reminders on your calendar schedule. These are scheduled and run entirely locally on your device.
  • Vibration Control: Enables haptic vibration alerts when a task alarm triggers.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Required to automatically reschedule your local notifications if you restart your phone.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Our app does not contain any third-party ads, analytics SDKs, trackers, or marketing tools. Your offline experience is completely self-contained and private.

4. Data Portability & Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You can export a JSON backup string of all tasks from the settings menu to move to another device. If you wish to delete your data, you can do so at any time by:

  • Using the "Clear & Reset Application" option in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app from your device (which automatically purges all locally stored storage).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the app is offline and doesn't communicate with us, we encourage you to check this page periodically for updates.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please contact our support at: support@taskflow.ai
